Frequently Asked

Questions,
Answered.

Practical guidance for keeping your CHARMENTIST jewellery in beautiful condition.

How often should I have my jewelry checked? +

For pieces worn frequently, we recommend having the jewellery inspected periodically, particularly if it contains gemstones or delicate settings. Contact our team if you notice any looseness, movement, or visible damage.

Can I clean my jewelry at home? +

For many pieces, gentle cleaning with a soft cloth is appropriate. If using water and mild soap, make sure the specific material and gemstone are suitable for this method. When in doubt, contact us before cleaning a delicate piece.

Can I use an ultrasonic cleaner? +

We do not recommend using ultrasonic cleaners without first confirming that the specific piece and gemstones are suitable. Certain gemstones, treatments, settings, and delicate constructions may be unsuitable for ultrasonic cleaning.

Which substances should I avoid? +

Avoid prolonged exposure to perfume, cosmetics, lotions, chlorine, household chemicals, and other harsh substances. Remove jewellery before swimming, exercising, showering, and applying beauty products.

What should I do if a gemstone feels loose? +

Stop wearing the piece and contact CHARMENTIST as soon as possible. Avoid attempting to tighten or repair the setting yourself. Our team can advise you on the appropriate assessment and next steps.

How do I request aftercare? +

Contact our team through the Contact page with your order information and a description of the care or service you require. We will guide you through the appropriate next step.
