Frequently Asked

Questions,
Answered.

Everything you need to know about caring for, maintaining, and receiving support for your CHARMENTIST jewellery.

How do I book a private consultation? +

Private consultations are available for clients who would like dedicated guidance before making a purchase. Contact us through our Contact page to enquire about availability and arrange a consultation.

How do I care for my jewelry? +
  • Storage: Store each piece separately in its pouch or box to help prevent scratches.
  • Protection: Avoid prolonged contact with perfume, lotion, water, and harsh chemicals.
  • Daily Cleaning: Gently wipe the piece with a soft, clean cloth after wearing.
  • Deep Cleaning: Where appropriate for the material and gemstone, use lukewarm water, mild soap, and a very soft brush. Dry thoroughly afterwards.
Does my jewelry come with a warranty? +

Every CHARMENTIST piece is accompanied by our Craftsmanship Guarantee, covering eligible structural and manufacturing defects according to the applicable guarantee terms.

  • Covered: Eligible manufacturing and setting defects.
  • Not Covered: Normal wear and tear, accidental damage, misuse, alteration, or loss.
  • Repairs: Eligible pieces may be assessed for repair, stone resetting, or refinishing.
Can I resize my ring? +

Many ring designs can be resized, although feasibility depends on the design, construction, gemstone setting, and material. Contact our team with your order information and we will assess the piece with you.

Can my jewelry be repaired? +

Repair requests are assessed individually. Depending on the condition and construction of the piece, available services may include structural repair, stone resetting, clasp servicing, or refinishing.

How do I request aftercare? +

Contact our team with your order details and a description of the service you require. We will guide you through the appropriate next steps.
