Questions,
Answered.

A few details about CHARMENTIST documentation, verification, and authenticity.

What documentation comes with my jewelry? +

CHARMENTIST pieces are accompanied by a Certificate of Authenticity and relevant product documentation. The exact information provided may vary depending on the piece and its materials.

Where is my documentation kept? +

Your documentation is included with your order. We recommend keeping the certificate and related records in a secure place for future reference.

Can I request documentation for an older piece? +

If you no longer have your documentation, contact CHARMENTIST with your original order information, certificate details, or other identifying information. We will review the available records and advise you on the next steps.

Does the certificate prove the value of my jewelry? +

The Certificate of Authenticity documents the identity and relevant characteristics of the piece. It is not, by itself, an independent appraisal or guarantee of resale value. For insurance or valuation purposes, an independent professional appraisal may be required.

How can I verify a CHARMENTIST piece? +

Contact our Client Services team with the piece's identifying information and available documentation. We can assist with checking the relevant purchase and product records.
