Our Approach
A Different Pace

Luxury made with intention, not excess.

CHARMENTIST approaches jewellery as something to be kept rather than quickly replaced. Our collections are developed with attention to material selection, construction, craftsmanship, and the life of each piece after it leaves the atelier.

By producing pieces in considered quantities and, where applicable, to order, we aim to avoid unnecessary production while giving greater attention to each creation.

We are a growing jewellery house, and our approach to responsible production continues to evolve alongside the brand.

The Journey of a Piece

Responsibility does not begin and end with sourcing. It continues through production, ownership, care, and the life of the jewellery itself.

01 / SELECT

Materials

Precious metals and gemstones are selected according to the requirements of each design and the standards we seek to maintain.

02 / CREATE

Craft

Skilled jewellery makers transform the selected materials through a considered process of construction, setting, polishing, and finishing.

03 / KEEP

Ownership

Each piece is designed to become part of a wardrobe, collection, or personal story rather than a temporary purchase.

04 / PRESERVE

Aftercare

Cleaning, resizing, repair, and restoration help extend the life of a CHARMENTIST creation for as long as possible.

Looking Forward

Responsibility is an ongoing practice.

CHARMENTIST is still a young jewellery house. We do not believe in presenting sustainability as a finished achievement. Instead, we see it as an ongoing process of making better decisions as our capabilities, partnerships, and understanding develop.
