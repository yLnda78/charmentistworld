Our Policy

One-Time Production, Considered Purchases

Each design is produced once, to order, within its Production Chapter. Because of this, we ask that measurements, materials and specifications be reviewed carefully before confirming an order with your Concierge.

Size & Style Exchanges

We offer exchange consultations for size adjustments or store credit toward another piece.

Bespoke & Custom Pieces

Custom, engraved, and one-of-a-kind (OOAK) commissions are final sale due to their personalized nature.

Quality Guarantee

If your piece arrives with a defect, contact us immediately for a prompt inspection and resolution.

Frequently Asked Questions
How do I initiate a return or exchange? +

Send us a message on WhatsApp with your order number and photos of the piece in its original packaging. Our team will guide you through the process.

Can I exchange for a different item? +

Because every piece is produced to order within its One-Time Production run, exchanges are considered case by case with your Concierge — typically for sizing, or for store credit toward a design from the current Production Chapter.

Are custom or bespoke pieces returnable? +

Bespoke, custom-sized, and engraved pieces are final sale, as they are crafted specifically for you.

Need Assistance?

Reach out via WhatsApp to start an exchange or repair request.
