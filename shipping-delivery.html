Delivery Details

Made to Order

Selected CHARMENTIST pieces are made to order. Production time varies by design and complexity, allowing each piece to receive the craftsmanship and finishing it requires.

Worldwide Delivery

CHARMENTIST ships internationally to most destinations through trusted express courier services, with tracking and appropriate shipment protection.

Discreet Packaging

Orders are placed inside secure, discreet outer packaging. Your CHARMENTIST presentation box is protected within.

Insured in Transit

Eligible shipments are insured for their declared value while in transit until delivery.

The estimated production time for each piece is displayed on its individual product page before purchase.

Shipping +

Once your piece has completed production and passed final quality inspection, it will be carefully prepared for dispatch.

Orders are shipped through trusted express courier services. Delivery time begins from the date your order is dispatched and varies according to destination, customs procedures, and local delivery conditions.

A tracking confirmation will be provided once your order leaves our atelier.

International Duties & Taxes +

International orders may be subject to import duties, taxes, customs fees, or other charges imposed by the destination country.

These charges are determined by the destination country's authorities and, where applicable, are the responsibility of the recipient.

Customs processing times may also affect the final delivery date.

Tracking & Delivery +

Once your order has been dispatched, you will receive tracking information by email.

Because CHARMENTIST pieces are valuable, certain shipments may require a signature upon delivery. Delivery requirements can vary depending on the destination and courier service.

Returns & Exchanges

Considered Purchases, Carefully Made

Because many CHARMENTIST pieces are produced specifically following an order, we ask that you carefully review the product details, measurements, materials, and specifications before completing your purchase.

Ready-to-Ship Pieces +

Ready-to-ship pieces purchased through the CHARMENTIST e-shop may be eligible for return or exchange within 7 days of confirmed delivery, provided that the item remains unworn, undamaged, unaltered, and in its original packaging.

Made-to-Order Pieces +

Made-to-order pieces are produced specifically following your purchase and are therefore generally final sale.

If your piece arrives damaged, incorrect, or materially different from the confirmed order specifications, please contact us as soon as possible so that we can review the matter with you.

For complete eligibility requirements, exclusions, and instructions, please refer to our Returns & Exchanges policy .

Frequently Asked
How long does my order take? +

Production time depends on the piece and is shown on its individual product page. Once dispatched, delivery time varies according to destination and customs processing.

Do you ship internationally? +

Yes. CHARMENTIST ships internationally to most destinations, subject to applicable shipping and customs restrictions.

How will I know when my order ships? +

You will receive a shipping confirmation with tracking information once your piece has completed production, passed final inspection, and been dispatched.

Can customs delay my delivery? +

Yes. International shipments may be subject to customs inspection or clearance procedures that are outside CHARMENTIST's control.

Is my shipment insured? +

Eligible shipments are insured during transit according to the applicable courier and shipping terms.

Do I need to be present for delivery? +

Depending on the destination and courier service, a signature may be required upon delivery.
