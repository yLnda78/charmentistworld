Privacy Policy

Updated: 2026

We respect your privacy and process personal details strictly to fulfill your orders, provide client support, and enhance your shopping experience.

1. Information We Collect

When you place an order or contact us for bespoke inquiries, we collect necessary personal details, including:

  • Contact details (Name, email address, phone number)
  • Shipping and billing addresses
  • Order records and custom design preferences
2. How We Use Your Information +

Your data is used solely to process orders, arrange secure delivery, issue authenticity documentation, and respond to your direct consultations.

3. Data Protection & Confidentiality +

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Details are shared only with essential payment and shipping partners to fulfill your purchase.

4. Your Privacy Rights +

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal records at any time by contacting our team via WhatsApp or email.
